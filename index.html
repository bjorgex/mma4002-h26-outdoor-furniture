Traced straight from the CAD: one flat bar runs from the ground up each end, bends into the seat rail, and — with the backrest selected — keeps going into a diagonal brace that carries the back planks. No separate foot, no cast parts; the bar reaches the ground on its own.

Three seat planks and, with a backrest, three more along the brace, each a single board dropped into a slotted pocket in the frame — no tenons, no spacer cubes. The centre seat plank is the one that takes the laser-cut mark or the logo shield. Sizes shown match the CAD demonstrator model as modelled, not a finished production specification.
